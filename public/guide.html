1Getting started

You skipper a little fishing boat with a net on a line behind it. Steer through the fish, fill your hold, sail home to sell, and spend the coins on a wider net, a bigger hold and a faster engine. A bigger boat sails further, and further out there are islands, sea monsters, a castle and a whole ocean of tar.

Your first few minutes

  1. Tap Cast off. You start next to the home dock in a dinghy that holds 12 fish.
  2. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer. Sail through a school of fish and the net scoops them up.
  3. When the hold bar goes red, a gold arrow points home. Sail into the ring by the dock and your fish sell by themselves.
  4. Spend your coins in the shop: Wider net, Bigger hold and Faster engine.
  5. Sail into floating driftwood on the way. It builds your treehouse, which grows into a palace.

You can't lose your stuff. Coins, upgrades and your boat are always kept. Sinking or losing a fight only ever costs the fish in your hold.

Stuck? Every place and boss below has a hint. If the hint isn't enough, open Show the answer.

Show the usual order of the story

  2. Island 2: the spear, the monkey camp and the tower.

  4. Island 4: the chemistry suit and the Tar Anchorer. The far deep opens.
  5. Buy the harpoon. Island 5: the Skeleton Shark King.
  6. Island 6: the Deep One and what's under it, Star, and the Reaper's egg. The Reaper at sea gives you an ice fishing kit for the ice floe.

  8. Islands 8 and 9: the Heron.
  9. Island 10: the old man's store, the swamp, the pelican and the crows.
  10. A crow shows you the way past the white buoys to island 11.
  11. Back in Gigantis: the crypt behind the skull door.
  12. The abyss, the metal bridge, the tar ocean and the tar island.
  13. Skull Island and Skrill.
  14. The Endless Isle.
  15. The Garden Isle, any time once the far deep is open. It's on the way to the Endless Isle.

Later on, the orb on the roof of the metal bridge always tells you what to do next.

The boat carves round rather than turning on the spot, so start your turns early. You can steer while the shop is open: drag on its wood and it fades out of the way.

On a keyboard, the ⌨ drive pill at the top changes how the keys feel. In drive, left and right turn the boat, up is the throttle and down is the brake. In point, the keys point the boat the way a thumb does.

What's on the screen

  • Top left: your coins, the hold (how many fish out of how many), and the order card. In a tower, on the Battle Barge, in the tunnels or out on the tar, the way out (Leave the tower, Back to the boat and so on) sits under your coins instead. In a battle on the Endless Isle, it's the score. On the ski run, it's Stop the run, with the clock under it.
  • Top right: sound on or off (♪), and Start over (↻). Start over takes two taps and wipes your game, so write your save code down first.
  • Down the right: chips for your hearts (ashore), the boat's hull (past the buoys), the time of day, driftwood, junk and snacks.
  • Bottom left: the round Sea map button. Once you own a spear, the crossed swords of the Battle Barge sit beside it.
  • Bottom right: the radar, once you've bought one.
  • Right edge: the round Throw button. It turns blue when the harpoon has something to hit. Other buttons pop up around it when you can use them.
  • Arrows at the edges point at things off the screen. Gold points to the dock when you're full, or to the fish your order wants. A coloured, pulsing marker is an island you haven't found yet: follow it.

3Fishing and coins

The net

Your net is towed on a line behind the boat, like a trailer. Turning swings it out wide, so sweep big curves through the schools. It only catches while it's moving along, and only while the hold has room. Schools thin out as you fish them and fill back up after a while.

About one catch in twenty is junk: an old boot, a tin can, a soggy sock. The dock won't buy it, but the muffin man will take it (see Side quests).

Selling and orders

Sail into the ring at a dock and the shop opens and sells your fish, cheapest first. Docking also mends a torn net and shakes out jellyfish. The islands you find later have docks that buy fish too.

The order card asks for a number of one kind of fish, like "12 mackerel pays 40". Catch them for the bonus and a new order. The gold arrow points to the nearest school it wants.

Driftwood floats all over the sea: sail into a log to pick it up. Salvage crates pay coins straight away. The fishmonger pays double for one kind of fish each day (his sign and a chip say which), and the smokehouse pays half as much again for tuna, goldfin and shark.

Day and night

A day lasts five minutes. At night the sea goes dark except around your boat and the lights. Moonfish and starfin only come out at night, and they glow. Every dawn brings one sunrise koi (60 coins) and every dusk one dusk ray (90): they sparkle, and they don't stay long. Lots of things come back each dawn, like treasure, honey and the day's special fish. Later you'll find places to sleep till morning.

Trouble near home

  • Jellyfish: a net full of jelly stops catching until you dock. The shipwright's fine mesh lets them slip through.
  • Sharks charge a moving net near the tuna, goldfin and lanternfish. Below net level 4 they tear it for a few seconds and fish spill out. From level 4 you catch them instead (40 coins each).
  • The pirate turns up once you've earned 60 coins. He chases a boat carrying 4 or more fish and steals half the hold. He never comes close to the dock and never goes past the buoys. The strongbox, the coast guard radio and your dog's bark all help.
  • Lionfish live further out and cut your net. The shipwright sells a mending kit, a rod that catches them for a bounty, and a net they can't cut.

Friendly things help too. Pass a dolphin at speed and it escorts you, and sharks leave you alone while it does. Ease up slowly beside a sea turtle and it swims with you (the net never takes turtles). Gulls circle over schools that still have fish.

Storms

Past the buoys, a storm comes along every so often, with a warning first. During it the boat is slower and the fish go deep. Afterwards they boil back up, and for a minute every fish counts double. Storms never reach home water.

Your hull and your hearts

Past the buoys a hull bar shows. Monsters bash it; home water and any dock mend it. If it reaches zero the boat is swallowed, and you come back up near home (or at your nearest base) with an empty hold and nothing else lost.

Ashore you have hearts: 3, or more with armour. A bonk takes one. Hearts come back slowly once a fight is over, and all at once aboard. Run out and you're bonked out: you wake on your boat, and anything you'd beaten stays beaten.

5The sea and its places

The sea comes in rings. Home water sits inside the first buoys, where nothing can hurt your boat. Past them is the deep, then the far deep behind a second line of buoys, then the far far sea past the tall white buoys. Beyond the far far sea, behind a metal bridge, lies an ocean of tar.

The sea map (bottom left) shows everywhere you've sailed. The rest is fog until you go there, and islands get their names once you find them. The places below are in the order you'll usually meet them.

Home island where you start

The pier and the dock, your palace tree, the muffin man's bakery and a street sign that says Drury Lane. A bridge on stilts runs off the west shore to a little beach: the Ten Cent Bridge. An otter lives in the water just north of the dock. While you're docked, tap Step ashore to walk around.

Show the answer
  • Build the palace one stage at a time with driftwood (see Fishing and coins). The first stage brings a dog. Tap him to pet him; he barks when the pirate's coming.
  • The otter: with fish in your hold, sail close and tap him. After three fish he's yours.
  • Something big lives under the far end of the Ten Cent Bridge, at dusk, night and dawn. See The snook in Side quests.
  • After the tree platform, an aquarium plot appears south of the palace tree.
Island 2 the deep, to the left

Only the flagship can cross the buoys. Once you're over, a green marker points the way. Island 2 has a trading post, a tower, parrotfish in its shallows, and a monkey camp on the far side. Finding it puts the spear on the shipwright's shelf.

Show the answer
  • Buy a spear and spear the parrotfish from the sand. They come back.
  • The monkey camp has five monkeys in skull masks. Three run at you and two throw coconuts: a shadow shows where each will land. Two hits each, or one with the barbed spear. Beat all five and the chest opens for 400 coins. The first time, you also free someone from a bamboo cage.
  • Climb the tower: two floors of monkeys, then the boss on the roof (see Bosses).
  • Build a base here: a hut (30 driftwood, 1,500 coins) makes it a home port, and a gear shed (50 driftwood, 3,000 coins) gives you the seine.
Island 3 the deep, at the top

After island 2, a pale blue marker leads up to the top of the map. A town lies drowned on the sea floor, and beside a tower that stands out of the water there's a floating town of rafts. Its dock buys fish, and ashore you can walk the planks right round the tower.

Show the answer
  • Climb the tower: two floors of monkeys, then a familiar face on the roof. The prize is the scuba gear, and a warning that something is down below.
  • With the scuba gear, walk round the tower's foot to a gap in the seaweed and tap Dive. The swordsman is waiting, and then something stranger (see Bosses).
  • Build a base: a hut (30 driftwood, 1,500 coins), and a crab shed (50 driftwood, 3,000 coins) for crab pots.
  • Groupers swim on the far side from the town.
Island 4, the tar island the deep, at the bottom

At first it's a plain little island nobody lands on. Something has to happen there first, and when it does you'll know. After that you'll need special gear to get in.

Show the answer
  • Once you free the prisoner under island 3, a scene plays the next time you're aboard, and island 4 turns to tar.
  • No boat can sail into its black water. Buy the chemistry suit (5,000) from the shipwright, sail up to the tar, almost stop, and tap Into the tar. You swim slowly through it and walk on its sand.
  • The Tar Anchorer rises the moment you step ashore (see Bosses). Beat him and the far deep opens.
The trench the deep, up and right of home

A patch of black-blue water with bubbles coming up, between home and island 3. You need scuba gear: stop over it and dive. It's very deep, and only glowing things show at the bottom. Watch your air bubbles. Running out just floats you back up, with nothing lost.

Show the answer
  • Treasure, once each, glinting in the dark: an old spyglass (200) and a ship's bell (250) on the right wall, a gold crown (500) and a glowing gem (400) on the left, and a treasure chest (600) on the floor.
  • A giant clam on the floor opens on a pearl worth 150 that grows back every dawn.
  • With a spear: glow squid (45), neon dragonfish (65) and lanternfish (22).
  • Something hunts the midnight water at the bottom. See The lurker in Bosses.
The far deep past the dark red buoys

Past the end of the deep stands a second line of dark red buoys. An anchor chain sags between them and nothing gets past it. Something on island 4 is holding it.

Show the answer

Beat the Tar Anchorer on island 4 and the chain sinks for good. The far deep has marlin at its four corners, islands 5 to 10, the ice floe, and some very big creatures.

Island 5, the island of bones far deep, upper left

A bone-white marker points the way. A sand bar of bleached bone, a giant shark's jaw, an empty throne, a wrecked boat on the rocks, and fins circling. There's nowhere to land. Buy the harpoon before you go close.

Show the answer
  • Come near the reef and the Skeleton Shark King comes for you (see Bosses). Sail far enough away and he gives up.
  • With scuba gear, stop near the wreck and tap Dive to the wreck. The chest in its hold holds 1,200 coins, the most of any treasure. Bone sharks cruise outside: when a jaw opens, get out of the way, or sting one off with your spear. Inside the hold they can't reach you.
Island 6, the big island far deep, upper right

A green marker appears once the Shark King is beaten. It's huge: several screens of walking, a long pier, a rocky hill, a green temple half in the sea, birds everywhere, and treasure hidden around it. Something sleeps off the temple. Something else sleeps in a cove.

Show the answer
  • Six treasure chests are hidden around the island, 300 coins each.
  • The Deep One sleeps at sea off the temple. Beat it and you'll find out what's under the temple. If you're bonked out down there, tap Into the temple at the temple door to go back.
  • Star, a flying meteor serpent, hunts flagships near the island.
  • The Reaper on land sleeps with his egg in a cove on the north-west side, behind the hill from the pier.
  • Build a base: a hut, and a charter office for taking passengers sightseeing (see Side quests).
The ice floe far deep, at the top

Old sea ice with a hole, a red flag and two seals. You can't land a boat on it, but stop alongside and see what you can do. To fish there you need an ice fishing kit, and someone big is carrying one.

Show the answer
  • The Reaper at sea spits out the ice fishing kit the first time you drive him off. Then stop alongside the floe and tap Ice fish (see Side quests).
  • Build an igloo (30 driftwood, 1,500 coins): a home port, and a bed to sleep till morning.
  • A seal lies at the edge. See Friends.
  • With scuba gear, tap Dive under the ice. Buy the white scuba suit (6,000), or the cold pulls you up after 15 seconds. Five frozen treasures to chip out: a spyglass by the hole (200), a chest at the back of the cave (800), a blue gem on the left ridge (400), a gold crown in the tunnel roof (500), and a ship's bell on the right ridge (250).
Island 7 far deep, lower left

A lavender marker appears after a big fight under island 6. It's like island 2, only bigger: a beach dock, a tower, a monkey camp, and a dark metal ring standing on burnt grass.

Show the answer
  • The monkey camp has six monkeys and a chest of 800 coins.
  • Climb the tower: two floors, then the sorcerer for the third time (see Bosses).
  • Beat it and the ring lights up green: it's a portal. Walk into it and tap Into the portal.
Gigantis, the castle on the sea through island 7's portal

A castle under a green sky, full of the undead. Every hit is one heart. Clear a room and its door opens, with your hearts filled. Leave, and the rooms fill up again next time, so try to do it in one go.

Show the answer
  • Skeletons: 4 hits. The sword goes up just before they slash, and the shield blocks your first spear.
  • Archers stay back and shoot where you're going. Change direction when an arrow flies.
  • Zombies: 8 hits, and slow. Their arms go up before they swing.
  • Ghosts: 3 hits. Their slow wisps drift after you: just walk away from them.
  • Necromancers: 6 hits. They raise skeletons from cracks in the floor, and the skeletons fall when they do. Spear them first.
  • The rooms go: the courtyard, the first hall, the second hall, then the throne room and the Forgotten One (see Bosses).
  • After him, try the chained door behind the throne with his bones beside you. Behind it are the ruins. In the ruins, a skull door needs a key from somewhere far away.
Islands 8 and 9 far deep, lower right

A blue marker appears once island 7's sorcerer is beaten. Twin islands with a sandbar between them, one dock (on island 8) and two towers. Midnight-blue boats patrol the water, and they'll ram you.

Show the answer
  • The hired boats: outrun them (the flagship is faster), or harpoon one 3 times for 60 coins. They go home for good once the Heron is beaten.
  • Camps of hired men on both islands: 3 hits each, 25 coins each, and 300 for clearing a camp.
  • Island 8's tower: two floors, then the Heron on the roof. He flees to island 9, and its tower opens.
  • Island 9's tower: two more floors, then his nest (see Bosses).
Island 10 far deep, far right

A pale green marker appears once islands 8 and 9 are found. A prairie with bunnies and bison, a pond, a forest, a swamp, and an old man in a rocking chair telling dad jokes. Most of the animals are friendly. Some aren't.

Show the answer
  • Tap the old man for his store: snacks, bug spray, honey, the treasure finder, badges and the treasure submarine.
  • The pond: Cast into the pond for peaceful fishing.
  • The swamp: gators in the bigger pools, a giant spider's web between two cypress trees, and a friendly swamp monster. The spider can't be speared, so bring bug spray.
  • The forest: gorillas, and a beehive with one free jar of honey a day.
  • The tower in the middle goes straight up to the giant pelican. Beat it for a loaf of bread.
  • Put the bread in the birdfeeder past the old man's porch, and three crows come, each with a question (see Side quests).
The white buoys past the far deep

A third line of buoys, tall and white with blinking lamps. The flagship is held there. "Sailors used to take a bird along and let it go, because a bird flies for land."

Show the answer

Win a crow at island 10's birdfeeder by answering its science question. Sail out to the white buoys with it aboard, tap Let a crow go, and follow it. It flies to island 11. After that the far far sea is always open.

Island 11 far far sea, past island 10

A big island, all jungle, with black water inland and old grey stones among the trees. Someone in a clearing wants a word.

Show the answer
  • In the clearing on the left waits the Heron's brother (see Bosses). Afterwards Caterman comes out of his workshop and joins you.
  • With scuba gear, tap Dive into the lagoon: murky water, ten lagoon creatures, and a skull key in the deepest part.
  • The stone portal on the far side stays dark until something in Gigantis's ruins is beaten. Then it takes you straight there.
The abyss far far sea, next to island 11

A round patch of the brightest blue with a dark middle. You need scuba gear: stop over the middle and dive. It's 1,000 metres down, and you go in a cage.

Show the answer
  • On the way down, giants swim past and slam the bars. Nothing is lost in the cage.
  • At the floor the door opens. Your air only runs outside the cage. Tap Ride the cage up whenever you like.
  • Spear the ten deep fish (91 coins each), the silver Remoro (190 coins, three spears, one a day) and the attack nibbler (150 coins, three spears, one a day).
  • A gulper hunts over the floor: swim aside when it gapes, or get back in the cage.
  • A full hold of abyss fish sells for about 10,000 coins.
The metal bridge far far sea, top right

A huge metal building on legs stands across the sea. Beyond it, the water is tar. Stop at the door in the middle.

Show the answer
  • Inside: a bunk and a mirror, the scientist (he buys tar fish and hands out missions), the tar dock, the missions room and the tar news.
  • Start mission 1, the stealth mission (see Side quests). Win it for the chemistry boat, which sails on tar.
  • Go up the ladder to the roof and put your hand in the orb. It shows your boats, every boss you've beaten, and what to do next.
The tar ocean and the tar island past the metal bridge

Only the chemistry boat sails the tar, and the tar ocean is as big as the whole sea. Out there the map button shows a tar map. The tar island has a tower on a hill, and things made of tar.

Show the answer
  • Tar fish: stop by a school and tap Cast for tar fish. Only the scientist buys them: 250 coins each, 12 a day.
  • Red tentacles rise in your way while you sail (see Bosses).
  • Step ashore at the end of the tar island's jetty. Tar humans walk the shore, tar beasts the hill, the tar leader stands by the tower, and a tar dragon circles the south-east slope. The tower door stays barred, for now.
  • Mission 2 at the bridge is the tar eel. Beat it, and something under the middle of the tar wakes up.
Skull Island far far sea, at the bottom

Its little skull marker shows once island 11 is found. It's the biggest island yet, shaped like a skull lying face up, and the pits in its eyes and nose have no bottom. A tower stands on its crown, and someone up there is yelling.

Show the answer
  • Skrill's men in bone armour, and displacer beasts, are everywhere (see Bosses).
  • Climb the tower: four floors, with your hearts filled after each. Then Through Skrill's door.
The Endless Isle far far sea, on the left

Its marker, a crossed cutlass and katana, shows once island 11 is found. Two halves that mirror each other, pirates on one and ninjas on the other, who have been fighting forever. Each half has its own pier.

Show the answer
  • Captain Cappy waits at the pirates' pier (Talk to Captain Cappy), and Master Sensei at the ninjas' (Bow to Master Sensei). Ask them why they're fighting.
  • Join either side, or both. The pirates give you Jacks Parrow, and the ninjas give you Bun-San (see Friends).
  • Once you've joined a side, its leader offers two battles, Fight: Capture the Flag and Fight: Hold the Forts, then asks where you'll fight. Be the tank (Quartermaster or Stone Guard, 14 hearts and armour, so every hit on it is a heart less), the damage (Swashbuckler or Shadow, 8 hearts) or the healer (Ship's Cook or Blossom Monk, 7 hearts, who throws a sparkly magic fish or pink mochi from up to 220 away, so stay back behind your tank). It's five a side: you and four bots against five bots. Your armour, spear and other friends sit it out, but your side's minion fights with you. Your basic attack is on the round button at the right: a blade for the tank and the damage, 2 a hit, and a throw for the healer, 1 a hit. You have armour too, in any place: every hit on you is a heart less, but never less than half a heart.
  • Each place comes with its Badge I ability, on the round button above the blade (or E). It aims itself, then takes a few seconds to be ready again. The tank's shout (Oi! Over here! or Mountain Shout) makes every enemy close by come at you for 4 seconds. The damage's shot (Flintlock or Shuriken) hits the nearest enemy up to 300 away for 4, after a red aim line. The healer's Sea Biscuit or Green Tea gives 4 hearts back to the most hurt teammate near you, or to you. The bots have theirs too.
  • Every battle gives marks to the place you fought in, on that side: 2 for a win, 1 for a draw or a loss. At 3 marks that place has Badge II, and at 8 Badge III, each with one more ability on its own button (R, then F). The badge card shows each place's badge and how many marks the next one takes. The bots in your battle have as many badges as you have in your place.
  • Badge II: the tank's Batten the Hatches or Iron Skin halves the hits on you for 6 seconds. The damage's Swing In or Shadow Step jumps you to an enemy up to 220 away and strikes for 3. 7Friends and pets

Friends follow you ashore everywhere, into every tower and room, and fight whatever is near you. If one gets worn out, it goes back to the boat to rest until you land again. Some of them sail their own little boats beside yours; the shop's Boats card says who rides what. The hint says what a friend does; the answer says how to get it.

The shop's Your pets card has everyone who is yours, pets and friends, with where each came from and where it is now. Tap Rename to give one a name of your own. The crows and the leggy muffins come as a crowd, so they keep theirs.

Draw a pet, at the bottom of the pets, opens a drawing pad. Draw your pet with your finger in six colours; Undo takes back the last line. Draw its legs as separate lines, low down, and it walks on them. The ink pot empties as you draw, and when it's dry the pen stops, so keep it simple. Tap Done, give it a name, and it's in your book, walking. You can have four drawn pets at once; to draw a fifth, let one go to live on the island.

Tap Take it along on a pet you drew and it walks with you: at your heel on every island and in every room, and riding at the back of your boat at sea. One pet walks with you at a time. Tap it to pet it.

Eggs ride in a nest crate on your boat, three at most (the Garden Isle will sell them once its coop opens). They hatch in real time, even while the game is closed: a common egg in 10 minutes, a rare one in an hour, a mythic one in 4 hours. Your pet book shows how long each has left. When one is ready it hatches the next time you're aboard and nothing dangerous is near: it shakes, it cracks, and out pops a pet of your own to name. What's inside is a surprise, but a pet from an egg can walk with you like one you drew.

The pet house is built from the shop once the tree platform is up, for 30 driftwood and 1,500 coins, on the plot with the paw sign by the hut. When it opens, its keeper gives you an egg. Every pet of your own that isn't walking with you lives there and plays in the yard in front of it; tap one to pet it. Walk up to the house to visit them in your pet book. Nobody there ever gets hungry or sad.

Training. Pets living at the pet house train there, a little every minute, even while the game is closed, from level 1 up to level 10. The one walking with you doesn't train, so leave a pet at the house to level it up. Time away counts for a day at most: a week away is the same as a day. Your pet book shows each pet's level and how close it is to the next, and the game tells you who levelled up while you were away.

Pet battles. Once the pet house is built, a sandy ring sits on the grass by the hut. Stand in it and tap Pet battle to fight a trainer's pet with one of your own. The first trainer is Pip and her sea fox, Scamp. A pet from an egg fights the way its kind does. A pet you drew picks how it fights the first time, for good: speedy, strong or tough, and a special (pounce, spit, roar or curl). In the ring you steer your pet with the stick, tap Bite when you're close (or press E or Space), and use its special (or R). When the ground goes red, Scamp is about to slam down there: get out of the red before it lands. Win, and your pet gets experience and you get a few coins. Lose, and nothing is lost: your pet is back at the pet house, fine already.

The ladder. After Pip come Moss, Wren, Ida, Old Fen and, last, Cora the champion, each waiting until you have beaten the one before. Each one's pet has its own trick: Moss's hedgehog rolls at you down a red lane, Wren's bunny takes turns slamming and rolling, Ida's frog is quick, and Old Fen's moth slams a much bigger patch of ground. The card says which level each is for. Beat a trainer and their ribbon hangs on your pet house; beat the champion and her cup stands beside it. A pet that curls up can take a slam on its spikes instead of running. The friends you've won can fight too: the dog barks, the otter splashes, Neil the seal curls up in his blubber, and the crows peck. They don't live at the pet house, so they only get better by fighting.

10Saving and other devices

  • It saves by itself, in this browser, every few seconds while you sail. Being ashore isn't saved, so a reload puts you back aboard.
  • Your save code is four words. The game backs itself up online under it, and once a week it shows you the code when you start. Write it down somewhere safe.
  • Play on another device (a card in the shop) shows your code with a Copy button, and a link with your whole game in it. On the other device, put the code or the link in the box and tap Load. It shows you both games before anything changes.
  • Install it with Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and works with no signal. On an iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own save, separate from Safari: move your game across with the save code.
  • Updates arrive by themselves. If one comes in the middle of a trip, the shop says "A new version is ready" at the next dock, and your game is saved before it updates.
  • Start over (↻, two taps) wipes your game. Write your code down first.

For grown-ups: add #tester to the end of the game's link for a tester panel that skips story steps and adds coins or friends. It changes the real save, so a skipped game stays skipped. #tester-off puts it away.
